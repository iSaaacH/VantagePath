# Third-party field asset

`assets/override-field.webp` is derived from **V5RC Override — Head-to-Head,
Top Down** by Jerry Lum, obtained from
[field-rendering.jerryio.com](https://field-rendering.jerryio.com/) and used
under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The original 4000 × 4000 PNG was composited onto an opaque background, cropped
to the exact 144-inch foam Floor, resized to 2048 × 2048, and re-encoded as
WebP. The crop and scale were calibrated in the separate
[`iSaaacH/vex-pathing`](https://github.com/iSaaacH/vex-pathing) project. The
floor edges map directly to Studio coordinates `(0, 0)` through `(144, 144)`;
there is no image offset or independent scale constant.
