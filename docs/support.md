# Support

Before reporting a control problem, collect:

1. the exact VantagePath commit or release;
2. wheel diameter, effective track width, gear ratio, and units;
3. configured `kS`, `kV`, and `kA` for both sides;
4. reference and measured pose;
5. target and measured wheel speeds;
6. commanded and available voltage;
7. actual loop period and final `FollowerStatus`.

Search existing issues or open a reproducible report on
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/iSaaacH/VantagePath/issues).

!!! tip

    A short CSV log and a minimal two-waypoint trajectory are much easier to
    diagnose than a video of a full autonomous routine.
